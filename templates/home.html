What makes it smart

🗣️

Multilingual chatbot + voice

Understands English, Hindi (देवनागरी) and Hinglish. Replies in the same language and can read replies aloud.

🧠

Explainable AI brain

A live panel shows exactly why the AI chose a department — top keywords, confidence and the severity breakdown.

🔗

Duplicate & repeat detection

Similar complaints from the same area are linked and boost priority. A problem that returns after "resolved" is flagged.

🚨

Multi-level escalation

Main Office → citizen confirmation → CM Helpdesk with an auto-generated accountability report.

📈

Predictive hotspots

Repeated issues at one place trigger early warnings — e.g. "possible pipeline failure in Vijay Nagar".

🏅

Department scorecards

Every department gets a performance grade from resolution time, escalations, reopen rate and ratings.
